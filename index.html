The problem

Your riding shoes are the wrong shape.

Nearly every flat-pedal shoe is built on a tapered last with a stiff midsole and 18 to 22 mm of foam under your heel. Toes get pinned together, feet go numb on long descents, and you feel the shoe instead of the pedal.

Barefoot shoes fixed the shape years ago, but they weren't built for pedals: no rubber that grips pins, no protection for rock gardens. TALUS is the shoe that does both.
